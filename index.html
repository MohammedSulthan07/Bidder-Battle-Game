THE QUICK START GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY

Buy in, read the room, and don't pay treasure prices for a box of old socks.

01

OPEN THE UNIT

The auctioneer reveals a storage-unit card. It tells everyone how many items are inside, whether one item is flipped face-up, and when players may peek.

02

BUY IN

Starting with the player to the auctioneer's left, each player decides whether to pay $100 to join that auction. The auctioneer collects each buy-in.

03

BID OR PASS

Bids go up in $100 steps. The active player may raise the current bid or pass. The highest bidder wins the whole unit and its items.

04

REVEAL THE LOOT

The winning bidder pays the auctioneer and adds the haul to their private stash. Item values count toward the final score; event cards apply their effect.

05

CROWN THE CHAMPION

Every player takes the auctioneer role twice. At the end, the player with the most cash + item value wins.

Starting setup: 3–7 players · $5,000 virtual cash each · $100 buy-in · bids in $100 increments. If a winner cannot pay, they face a $1,000 in-game fine; if they cannot pay the fine, they are eliminated.

This digital edition uses original sample items and event effects. Bids are cash-only here. All amounts are pretend game currency; no real money or prizes are involved.
